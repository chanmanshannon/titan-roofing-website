Our Story
How Titan Got Started

Titan Roofing was founded in 2002 by Ed and Greg Brogdon — brothers born into a family of carpenters with sawdust in their veins and a handshake that still means something. Between them, they carry over three decades of construction experience, learned the old way: on job sites, from their father, one nail at a time.

Their vision was simple but stubborn — run a roofing company that does the same quality work on the 30,000th roof as it did on the first. No shortcuts. No excuses. A system rooted in consistency, where every crew member is trained to the same standard and every homeowner gets the same experience.

What makes Titan different isn't just experience — it's roots. Ed and Greg didn't move to Houston to chase storm work. They grew up here. They've raised their families here. And for 25 years, they've been installing roofs over the heads of their own neighbors. There's a good chance Titan originally roofed your home during new construction — and now they're the ones you call when it's time for a replacement.

That's not a sales pitch. That's just what happens when you stay in one place long enough, do good work, and treat people right.

CertainTeed SELECT ShingleMaster

The highest certification tier in the shingle roofing industry. Titan Roofing is proud to hold this designation, placing us in the top 1% of roofers nationwide.

What We Must Maintain

To hold our SELECT ShingleMaster certification, Titan must continuously meet rigorous requirements:

✓At least 50% of workforce must be Master Shingle Applicators, with at least one Shingle Quality Specialist
✓Strict adherence to Code of Ethics and Professional Practices
✓Current worker compensation and liability insurance at all times
✓Regular testing and re-accreditation on the Shingle Applicators Manual
✓Demonstrated product knowledge for client consultation
✓Minimum 5 years in business or 1 year as ShingleMaster
✓Company owner must pass Business Fiscal Responsibility exam
