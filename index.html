Does Your Roof Look Like This?

Click the red hotspots to see what's wrong — then click the green hotspots to see how Titan fixes it.

Algae & Discoloration

What you see: Dark streaks and discoloration across the roof surface.

What it leads to: Algae retains moisture, accelerating granule loss and shingle breakdown.

Granule Loss & Wear

What you see: Faded, uneven color with visible bare spots on shingle surfaces.

What it leads to: Shingles become brittle, crack, and lose waterproofing ability. Check your gutters for granule buildup.

Missing Shingles

What you see: Bare spots where shingles have blown off, exposing dark underlayment or decking.

What it leads to: Direct water entry. Rot, mold, and structural damage can begin within weeks.

Deteriorating Flashing

What you see: Worn, corroded, or separated flashing around the chimney base.

What it leads to: Penetrations are the #1 source of roof leaks. Failed flashing sends water into your walls.

Mismatched Patch Repairs

What you see: Different-colored shingles patched over damaged areas.

What it leads to: Patches rarely address underlying damage and create weak seams.

What Is a CertainTeed
SELECT ShingleMaster?

The SELECT ShingleMaster credential represents the highest standards in the shingle roofing industry and is offered by CertainTeed — a licensing body designed to assist homeowners in selecting a qualified contractor and premium roofing products. Titan Roofing holds this designation, placing us in the top 1% of roofers nationwide.
