Typical Contractor

BACK TO SQUARE ONE

Cheaper materials and faster installs might look fine on day one — but three-tab shingles, felt underlayment, reused pipe boots, and high-nailed fasteners tell a different story a decade later:

✗Three-tab shingles curling and cracking — thin, single-layer shingles without impact rating. UV degrades them in 8–12 years.
✗Felt underlayment has failed — absorbs water, tears easily, lasts only 12–20 years. Your shingles sit on nothing but bare wood.
✗Reused pipe boots are cracked and leaking — old rubber dries out in UV. Ceiling stains or attic mold are common signs.
✗High-nailed shingles blowing off — nails placed above the manufacturer's zone means shingles peel off in the first major wind event.
✗Warranty? Expired or voided. Basic manufacturer warranty only, and improper installation voids even that.

Under The Surface
Not All Shingles Are Created Equal

The difference between a typical contractor and a Titan roof starts right here — the shingle itself. Click each layer to see what you're really getting.

TYPICAL THREE-TAB SHINGLE

20-Year Basic Single-Layer Shingle

Standard Granule Layer No Algae Protection
Basic mineral granules with no algae treatment. In Houston's humidity, black streaks typically appear within 3–5 years.
No Impact Rating Hail Vulnerable
Single-layer construction provides no rated hail protection. Golf-ball hail fractures the mat and dislodges granules.
Single-Layer Construction Bare Minimum
One thin layer of fiberglass and asphalt. Significantly lighter and less durable — the cost savings come directly from less material.
Standard Asphalt UV Degradation
Basic asphalt without UV stabilizers. Dries out and becomes brittle in Houston heat within 8–12 years.
No Nailing Guide Install Risk
No printed guide means nailing zone is up to installer judgment. Crews routinely high-nail to work faster — voiding the warranty.
Basic Sealant Strip 60 MPH Wind
Standard adhesive rated for only 60 MPH — well below Houston's storm wind speeds. First major wind event can peel shingles off entirely.

CERTAINTEED LANDMARK

30-Year Laminated Architectural Shingle

Algae-Resistant Granule Layer StreakFighter
Ceramic-coated copper granules that actively prevent algae growth — the black streaks you see on older Houston roofs. Keeps your roof looking new for decades.
Class 3 Impact-Rated Surface Hail Protection
Engineered to withstand 1.75" hail (golf ball sized). Houston averages 4–6 significant hail events per year — this is the difference between filing a claim and not needing to.
Dual-Layer Laminated Construction Durable
Two layers of fiberglass mat bonded with weathering-grade asphalt. Thicker, heavier, and significantly more durable than single-layer three-tab shingles.
UV-Stabilized Asphalt Core UV Protection
Specially formulated asphalt that resists UV breakdown. Standard asphalt dries out and cracks in Houston summers — this stays flexible and waterproof year after year.
NailTrak Nailing Guide Patent
Printed nailing zone ensures nails are placed exactly where the manufacturer specifies. Eliminates high-nailing — the #1 cause of blow-offs and voided warranties.
Tru-Bond Sealant Strip 130 MPH Wind
Factory-applied adhesive bonds each shingle to the one below. Rated for 130 MPH wind resistance — critical for hurricane and derecho country.

It's Not About Price — It's About What You're Getting

A lower quote usually means cheaper materials and installation shortcuts — skipped valley protection, reused pipe boots, high-nailed shingles. Those shortcuts don't show up on day one, but they do show up when a valley leaks, boots crack, or shingles blow off in the next storm. And by then, your warranty won't cover it. The cheapest roof is the one you never have to replace twice.
