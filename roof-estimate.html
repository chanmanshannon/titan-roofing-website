Questions

Before you compare

How accurate is the online estimate?

When we can measure your roof from satellite imagery, the range is usually close to the final bid. If you enter your home size instead, the range is wider. The final price is set after a free inspection, which checks decking condition, the number of existing layers and ventilation.

Do I have to give my phone number to see a price?

No. You see your full estimate and quote comparison without entering any contact details. You only share them if you want a firm written bid.

Why can Titan sometimes beat another roofer's quote?

We've installed more than 30,000 roofs since 2002 with our own crews, not subcontractors, and most homes are finished in a single day. That volume and efficiency can let us price a better roofing system for the same money or less.

What if my other quote is lower than Titan's estimate?

Then compare what's included. Lower quotes often skip a full tear-off, synthetic underlayment, valley protection or new pipe boots. The estimator shows what those items would add so you can compare like for like. Want a second opinion? Download 7 Questions to Ask Before You Sign.

Does insurance change what I pay?

If storm damage qualifies for an insurance claim, you typically pay your deductible and the insurer pays the rest. We document the damage and meet your adjuster on site. How storm claims work →
