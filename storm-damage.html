When Insurance Pays for Your Roof

Most Houston homeowners don't realize their roof damage already qualifies for an insurance-covered replacement. Here are the two most common scenarios.

🧊

Hail Damage — Houston's Annual Problem

Greater Houston gets hit with golf-ball-sized hail nearly every spring. Hail strikes fracture shingles and knock off the protective granule coating. Once those granules are gone, the asphalt degrades rapidly.

When an adjuster sees widespread granule loss from hail, that's typically an automatic approval for a full roof replacement — minus your deductible.

Signs to look for:
→Black granules collecting in your gutters or at downspout bases
→Dark, sandy grit washing onto your driveway or patio after rain
→Circular dents or divots on shingles, visible in raking light
→Dents in aluminum gutters, vent caps, or AC unit fins
💨

Wind Damage — Derechoes, Hurricanes & Tornadoes

Houston faces severe wind events regularly — the 2024 Derecho, Hurricane Beryl, and seasonal tornadoes all generate winds that tear shingles off entirely.

Even if ground-level damage looks limited, a professional inspection often reveals hidden wind lift, broken seal strips, and cracked shingles that qualify for a covered claim.

Signs to look for:
→Shingles on the ground, in the yard, or missing from visible roof planes
→Curled or lifted shingle edges, especially on ridgelines
→Exposed dark underlayment or bare wood decking
→Damaged ridge caps, loose flashing, or leaning roof vents
